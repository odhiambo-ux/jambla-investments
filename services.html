SERVICES

Connectivity, installed
and supported properly.

Everything below is delivered by our own Kisumu-based technicians — no outsourced call centres, no guesswork installs.

01 — HOME INTERNET

Fibre where it runs, wireless where it doesn't.

Most estates in Milimani, Mamboleo, and Nyalenda are fibre-ready. Where fibre hasn't reached yet, our fixed wireless service delivers comparable speeds with no trench-digging or waiting on infrastructure rollout.

  • Unlimited data — no throttling after a cap
  • Router included with every plan
  • Free reconnection if you move within our coverage area
02 — BUSINESS INTERNET

Built for shops, offices, and hotels that can't afford downtime.

Business accounts get dedicated bandwidth, priority fault response, and the option of a static IP for hosting, VPNs, or point-of-sale systems.

  • Static IP available on request
  • Service Level Agreement with response-time guarantees
  • Backup wireless failover for critical operations
03 — WIFI INSTALLATION

A signal that reaches every corner, on purpose.

We don't just drop a router at the door. Every install starts with a walkthrough of your space to map dead zones, then places access points or mesh units to match how the building is actually used.

  • Free pre-install site survey
  • Mesh systems for multi-floor homes and offices
  • Separate guest networks for shops, cafés, and guesthouses
  • Cable management and clean install, not exposed wiring
04 — IT SUPPORT

The IT help small teams usually go without.

Most small offices in Kisumu don't have room in the budget for a full-time IT hire. Our support plans cover the everyday problems that eat up a workday — without the overhead.

  • Remote troubleshooting included with business internet plans
  • Printer, shared drive, and email setup
  • Same-day on-site visits within Kisumu CBD
  • Basic cybersecurity setup: firewalls, backups, staff guidance
